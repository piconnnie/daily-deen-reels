Before repeating something about someone, pause.

Sahih Muslim 2589: backbiting is mentioning your brother in a way he dislikes — and the hadith addresses the case where it is true.

Swipe for three kinder responses when the conversation turns to someone.

Share it with someone who may find it helpful.

📖 Sources
• Sahih Muslim 2589 (Sahih, tr. Abdul Hamid Siddiqui) — sunnah.com/muslim:2589
Full text: Abu Huraira reported Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) as saying: Do you know what is backbiting? They (the Companions) said: Allah and His Messenger know best. Thereupon he (the Holy Prophet) said: Backbiting implies your talking about your brother in a manner which he does not like. It was said to him: What is your opinion about this that if I actually find (that failing) in my brother which I made a mention of? He said: If (that failing) is actually found (in him) what you assert, you in fact backbited him, and if that is not in him it is a slander
