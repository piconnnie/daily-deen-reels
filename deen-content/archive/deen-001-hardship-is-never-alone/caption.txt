If this week is heavy, sit with these two ayat for a minute.

In Surah Ash-Sharh the same promise appears twice, one ayah after the other: with hardship, ease.

A reflection for today:
→ name what is hard, honestly
→ look for the ease that is already beside it
→ ask Allah for patience and for relief
