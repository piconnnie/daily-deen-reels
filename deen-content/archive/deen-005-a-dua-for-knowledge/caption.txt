A short du'a to begin your day with intention.

From Sahih Muslim 2722: the Prophet ﷺ used to seek refuge from knowledge that does not benefit.

Say it before studying, before work, or after Fajr.

Save this du'a.

📖 Sources
• Sahih Muslim 2722 (Sahih, tr. Abdul Hamid Siddiqui) — sunnah.com/muslim:2722
Full text: Zaid b. Alqam reported: I am not going to say anything but only that which Allah's Messenger (may peace be upgn him) used to say. He used to supplicate: " O Allah, I seek refuge in Thee from incapacity, from sloth, from cowardice, from miserliness, decrepitude and from torment of the grave. O Allah, grant to my soul the sense of righteousness and purify it, for Thou art the Best Purifier thereof. Thou art the Protecting Friend thereof, and Guardian thereof. O Allah, I seek refuge in Thee from the knowledge which does not benefit, from the heart that does not entertain the fear (of Allah), from the soul that does not feel contented and the supplication that is not responded
• Recitation: Qur'an 20:114 — Mishary Rashid Alafasy (EveryAyah.com) — quran.com/20/114
