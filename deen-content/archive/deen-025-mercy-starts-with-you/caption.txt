We all hope for mercy. This hadith asks us to start giving it.

Sahih al-Bukhari 6013 is one line long and asks a lot of us.

Try this today:
→ be patient with someone who is slow
→ forgive one small wrong without mentioning it
→ be gentle with children and animals

Share this with your family.

📖 Sources
• Sahih al-Bukhari 6013 (Sahih, tr. Muhsin Khan) — sunnah.com/bukhari:6013
Full text: Narrated Jarir bin `Abdullah: The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "He who is not merciful to others, will not be treated mercifully
