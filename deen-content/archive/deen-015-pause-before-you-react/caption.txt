Not every feeling needs an immediate reaction.

Sahih al-Bukhari 6114 redefines strength: controlling yourself when angry.

At work or at home today: pause before you reply.

Save this for your next hard moment.

📖 Sources
• Sahih al-Bukhari 6114 (Sahih, tr. Muhsin Khan) — sunnah.com/bukhari:6114
Full text: Narrated Abu Huraira: Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said, "The strong is not the one who overcomes the people by his strength, but the strong is the one who controls himself while in anger
• Recitation: Qur'an 3:134 — Mohamed Siddiq Al-Minshawi (Murattal) (EveryAyah.com) — quran.com/3/134
