Big bursts of worship feel great. Small daily ones last.

The deeds loved most by Allah are the regular ones, even if few (Sahih al-Bukhari 6465).

Pick one this week:
→ two rak'ah of sunnah you can keep every day
→ one page of Qur'an after Fajr
→ a daily sadaqah, even a small one
