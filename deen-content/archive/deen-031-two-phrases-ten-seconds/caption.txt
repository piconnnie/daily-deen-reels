Before the next video: two short phrases.

Sahih al-Bukhari 6406: easy on the tongue, heavy in the balance, dear to the Most Merciful.

Say them now, then once more on your way somewhere today.

Save this and say them daily.

📖 Sources
• Sahih al-Bukhari 6406 (Sahih, tr. Muhsin Khan) — sunnah.com/bukhari:6406
