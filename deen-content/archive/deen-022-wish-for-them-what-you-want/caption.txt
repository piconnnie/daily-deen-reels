When a friend gets the thing you wanted, what's your first feeling?

Sahih al-Bukhari 13 ties faith to wishing for your brother what you like for yourself.

Try this today:
→ congratulate someone sincerely
→ make du'a for a friend by name
→ pass on a good opportunity you found

Send this to a friend.

📖 Sources
• Sahih al-Bukhari 13 (Sahih, tr. Muhsin Khan) — sunnah.com/bukhari:13
Full text: Narrated Anas: The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "None of you will have faith till he wishes for his (Muslim) brother what he likes for himself
