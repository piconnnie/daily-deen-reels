Jumu'ah Mubarak. Have your du'a ready today.

Sahih al-Bukhari 935: an hour on Friday, and the Prophet ﷺ pointed out how short it is.

Today:
→ write down what you want to ask for
→ ask in your salah
→ include someone else in your du'a

Share it with your family.

📖 Sources
• Sahih al-Bukhari 935 (Sahih, tr. Muhsin Khan) — sunnah.com/bukhari:935
Full text: Narrated Abu Huraira: Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) talked about Friday and said, "There is an hour (opportune time) on Friday and if a Muslim gets it while praying and asks something from Allah, then Allah will definitely meet his demand." And he (the Prophet) pointed out the shortness of that time with his hands
